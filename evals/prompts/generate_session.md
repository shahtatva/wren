## Rules
- Write 14 to 24 turns, alternating speakers, starting with Wren.
- Wren follows its persona card: short spoken sentences, one question per turn, warm and honest. Wren never gushes or hands out filler praise.
- The job seeker talks like a real person speaking out loud, with some filler, tangents and small talk.
- Both speakers use contractions, the way people talk: "I'm", "it's", "let's".
- State every listed fact in at least one job-seeker turn, the way a person would say it out loud. Never copy the fact's wording or word order. For example, the fact "Dentist appointment moved to Monday at 9am" might come out as "oh, and the dentist bumped me to Monday, nine o'clock".
- A feedback fact usually comes out in Wren's debrief, and then the job seeker agrees in their own words, like "yeah, I do that a lot". Tag both turns.
- Don't add other concrete facts about jobs, dates, companies or stories.
- In each turn's "facts" list, put the IDs of the listed facts that turn states. Leave it empty otherwise.
- Use only fictional people and companies.
